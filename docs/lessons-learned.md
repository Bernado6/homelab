# Lessons Learned

Each step in the guide ends with a **💡 What I learned** note. These are the general lessons that cut across all of them.

1. **Goal first, hardware second.** Write down what you want to learn and run, and the specs follow.
2. **RAM is king.** CPU is rarely the bottleneck; RAM is. Buy upgradeable hardware.
3. **Start small, add in stages.** I'm running light apps on 8GB now and will add Immich and Nextcloud after a RAM upgrade.
4. **Wired beats wireless** for servers.
5. **Plan for power cuts.** AC Recovery → Power On on day one; a UPS later.
6. **Go headless quickly.** Monitor and keyboard for install, then SSH for everything.
7. **Keep it local first.** Don't expose anything to the internet until you understand HTTPS, authentication and VPNs.
8. **Backups before precious data.** Don't move photos onto a single SSD without a backup plan.
9. **Verify advice, including AI advice.** Several suggestions I received mixed incompatible tools or had outdated prices.
10. **Write it down as you go.** This guide started as my own notes. Documenting the journey made every step clearer.
